Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline Why does PEGASUS Phone fail to establish a wireless bridge on 5GHz?
expand_more
Wireless ADB daemon requires client and host isolation to be disabled on enterprise access points. Ensure mDNS multicast packet forwarding is unblocked on Port 5555. Alternatively, plug in the USB 3.2 cable to pair cryptographic tokens automatically before switching back to Wi-Fi mode.
help_outline How does Hardware KeyStore security keep my runtime isolated?
expand_more
PEGASUS Connect provisions an asymmetric TLS session where the private key is held exclusively in the Android TrustZone (StrongBox). No telemetry or raw sensor feeds leave the local bridge subnet without explicit hardware confirmation.
help_outline Can I export telemetry and crash reports directly to Datadog or Sentry?
expand_more
Yes. Navigate to Settings > Integrations > Webhooks. Configure your OTLP (OpenTelemetry Protocol) collector endpoint to pipe logcat buffers and CPU/GPU flamegraphs in real-time.
